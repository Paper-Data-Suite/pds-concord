# v0.3.1 Starter Subject Relationships

Issue #113 corrects starter Packet workflows where the person receiving and
completing a Packet is not necessarily the person or context the resulting
Artifact concerns. This document is normative for the package-owned starter
semantics introduced by #113.

## Identity model

Concord preserves three independent concepts:

```text
Packet target
    who receives/is targeted by one generated Packet

Artifact Author
    who completed, produced, recorded, or formally represented the Artifact

Artifact Subject
    whom or what the Artifact describes or concerns
```

The canonical Author/Subject authority remains ADR 0005. #113 does not add a
reviewer/reviewee persistence model, a generic student field, or relationship
identity to PDS2. Generation produces proposed Author/Subject associations
through the existing typed model.

### Self-participant starters

For the nine self-participant starters, the Package target, initial Author, and
primary Subject may intentionally coincide. References to partners, peers,
stations, or Group activity inside the form are evidence/context and do not
automatically create additional Subjects.

`Socratic Seminar Preparation & Notes` remains explicitly in this category.
Its discussion notes do not turn the form into an observer Artifact: the form is
the student's own preparation, note-taking, and reflection.

### Group starters

The sixteen Group starters retain Group-target / collective-Group-author /
Group-Subject semantics. Concord does not enumerate Group members as individual
Subjects merely because names or roles may appear on the paper.

### Observer starters

`fishbowl_observer` and `talk_moves_observer` use package-current v2 semantics:

```text
Packet target: observer student
initial Author: observer student
authorship mode: observer
Subject: selected Session
Subject role: session_context
```

No individual observed participant is inferred.

### Explicit peer-Subject starters

`peer_review_writing`, `peer_review_presentation`, and
`peer_design_code_review` require explicit teacher-reviewed Subject binding.

Writing accepts a Core student Subject. Presentation/Product and Design/Code
accept a Core student or an applicable Concord Group. Packaged v2 peer-review
contracts prohibit silently binding the reviewer to themself.

Generation proposes the reviewer as initial Author and the reviewed
student/Group as Subject with role `reviewed_subject`. Writing and
Presentation/Product permit several Authors semantically because a later human
attribution review may establish that a creator completed a response section.
Generation does not infer that second Author.

## Immutable v1 to v2 starter lineages

The five affected starters retain their exact historical v1 identities and
rendering bytes. Their package-owned v2 successor uses the same stable
`starter_key` and `template_id`, a new immutable Template Version identity,
revision sequence 2, explicit `supersedes_template_version_id`, and a separate
rendering asset.

Fresh installation of an affected starter establishes the complete package
lineage:

```text
v1 -> superseded historical Version
v2 -> active package-current Version
```

A workspace containing an exact packaged v1 reports `upgrade_available`.
Explicit install/update appends and activates v2 without rewriting v1. A
package-current lineage is an idempotent no-op. An incompatible
teacher-authored successor fails closed. Install-all preserves deterministic
partial-success/recovery behavior and reports installed, upgraded, and already
current counts separately.

Existing reusable Packet Versions are not rewritten to point at v2. An exact
Packet Version that references starter v1 continues to use v1 semantics.

## Subject binding

The reusable Template contract adds
`TemplateSubjectResolutionExpectation` without changing the serialized shape of
historical `TemplateSubjectExpectation`.

Explicit runtime mappings use noncanonical `PacketSubjectBinding` values:

```text
packet_component_id
target_key
subject_reference
```

The direct CLI transports these values in the strict Packet generation
`--options-file`. The ordinary teacher menu presents roster names and Group
labels and internally binds exact IDs. Neither interface infers pairings from
roster order, Group membership, Roles, random order, or previous assignments.

Preparation remains zero-write. Subject bindings participate in the generation
review digest and are revalidated at commit; stale roster/Group/context state or
changed pairings therefore fail before native generation mutation.

## Privacy and policy

Effective privacy is resolved from the actual proposed Subject, not from the
Packet target merely because that student holds the paper. Routine physical
fallback identity uses the #102 privacy-minimized student label:

```text
first name + last initial
```

Raw student IDs, emails, complete roster data, and Group member enumeration do
not enter PDS2, route module details, filenames, or ordinary physical fallback
text.

## Physical relationship identity

Package-current v2 rendering freezes role-aware values at generation time.

Peer-review examples:

```text
Reviewer: Alex One
Reviewee: Blair Two
```

or:

```text
Reviewer: Alex One
Reviewed: Group B
```

Observer example:

```text
Observer: Alex One
Observed: Seminar Session 1
```

The corresponding fallback remains privacy-minimized for students, for example:

```text
Class: english12-p2 | Reviewer: Alex O. | Reviewee: Blair T.
```

Relationship-aware v2 contracts permit a bounded 240-character human fallback;
historical v1 remains at its existing 160-character bound. Generation fails
before canonical mutation rather than clipping a relationship identity that
does not fit the exact Template Version contract.

Resolved non-route rendering values are frozen into
`PacketInstanceArtifactBinding.rendering_values`. Re-render of an existing
Packet never consults the current roster, Group label, or Session label.
A later roster rename, Group rename, Session rename, or semantic Subject
correction therefore cannot silently rewrite historical paper bytes.

## PDS2 and routing

PDS2 remains an Artifact Page locator. #113 does not put reviewer/reviewee
identity into the QR payload.

Route `module_details` remains exactly:

```text
activity_id
artifact_instance_id
artifact_page_id
page_number
```

`ArtifactPage.human_fallback` and `RouteRegistration.human_fallback` remain equal
once generated.

## Returned-work attribution

Generation relationships are proposals, not confirmations. Existing attribution
workflows remain authoritative after paper returns: a teacher can confirm,
dispute, add, remove, or replace Authors and Subjects based on the evidence.
Correcting semantic attribution does not rewrite the historical generated page.

## Compatibility guarantees

#113 requires no migration or reinterpretation of existing Template Versions,
Packet Versions, PacketInstances, Artifacts, Pages, Author/Subject associations,
routes, retained scans, reviews, Scores, PDS2 payloads, or rendered PDFs.
Historical v1 Template JSON continues to load, validate, and serialize to exact
canonical bytes.

Release qualification for #113 additionally requires representative historical
v1 replay/returned-evidence checks, a persistent five-starter visual-review
bundle with human approval, and isolated installed-wheel acceptance against the
released compatible Core wheel. Those qualification artifacts are acceptance
evidence; they do not alter the semantics defined here.
